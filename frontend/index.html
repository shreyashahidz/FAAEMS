Accreditation Evaluation Framework

Evaluation criteria across Professional Development and Research activities.

Professional Development Activities

  • Memberships in Professional Societies at National/International Levels
  • Faculty as Resource Persons or Participants in STTPs/FDPs
  • Faculty Contribution in Development of SWAYAM MOOCs and other E-Content
  • Faculty Certification of MOOCs through SWAYAM, etc.
  • FDP/STTP Organized by Department
  • Faculty Support in Student Innovative Projects
  • Faculty Internship/Training/Collaboration with Industry

Research and Development Activities

  • Academic Research & Refereed Publications
  • Ph.D. Student Enrolled & Graduated Supervision
  • Patents Granted, Published, and Working Prototypes
  • Sponsored Research Projects
  • Consultancy Work from External Sources
  • Institution Seed Money or Internal Research Grant to its Faculty for Research Work
