Vision

To become a department of international relevance in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

Mission

  • Provide quality education in AI & ML supported by state-of-the-art infrastructure, modern tools and agile methodologies.
  • Nurture strong technical knowledge and research aptitude through holistic and multidisciplinary education, empowering students to solve real-world problems and develop sustainable AI-driven solutions.
  • Enhance local and global employability by aligning the curriculum with industry requirements and fostering collaboration with industry, academia, and research organizations.
  • Inculcate leadership qualities, ethical values, communication skills, lifelong learning, and social sensitivity to develop responsible and globally competent professionals.

Program Educational Objectives (PEO)

Batch 2022-26, 2023-27 onwards, HME 2023

  • PEO 1Knowledge: Build a strong foundation in computing, artificial intelligence, and research methodologies to adapt to emerging technologies.
  • PEO 2Skills: Develop innovative and intelligent solutions for real-world and societal challenges through continuous skill enhancement.
  • PEO 3Abilities: Demonstrate leadership, effective communication, ethical values, and a commitment to lifelong learning as globally competent professionals.

Program Outcomes (PO)

Batch 2023-27, 2024-28 onwards

  • PO 1Engineering Knowledge: Apply knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.
  • PO 2Problem Analysis: Identify, formulate, research literature, and analyze complex engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences.
  • PO 3Design / Development of Solutions: Design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet specified needs with appropriate consideration for public health, safety, and cultural, societal and environmental factors.
  • PO 4Investigations of Complex Problems: Use research-based knowledge and research methods including design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of information to provide valid conclusions.
  • PO 5Engineering Tool Usage: Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources and modern engineering and IT tools, including prediction and modelling, with an understanding of their limitations.
  • PO 6The Engineer and the World: Apply reasoning informed by contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to professional engineering practice.
  • PO 7Ethics: Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics, responsibilities and norms of engineering practice.
  • PO 8Individual & Collaborative Team Work: Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams and in multi-disciplinary settings.
  • PO 9Communication: Communicate effectively on complex engineering activities, comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, and make effective presentations.
  • PO 10Project Management & Finance: Demonstrate knowledge of engineering and management principles and apply these as a member and leader in a team to manage projects in multidisciplinary environments.
  • PO 11Life-Long Learning: Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability to engage in, independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSO)

Batch 2022-26, 2023-27 onwards, HME 2023

  • PSO 1Technical Competency: Demonstrate proficiency in mathematics, computing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science by utilizing modern engineering tools and techniques.
  • PSO 2Problem Solving: Analyze engineering problems and apply AI and ML techniques to develop innovative and sustainable solutions for industrial and societal applications.
